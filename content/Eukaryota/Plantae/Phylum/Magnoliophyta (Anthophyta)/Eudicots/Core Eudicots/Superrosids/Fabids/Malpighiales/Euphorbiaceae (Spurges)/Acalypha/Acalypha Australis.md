# Acalypha Australis

## Quick ID (field-use)
- Leaf type: Deciduous, broadleaf
- Leaf arrangement: Alternate
- One-liner ID clue: Low-growing annual with serrated, ovate leaves and slender green axillary flower spikes

## Taxonomy
- Family: Euphorbiaceae
- Genus: Acalypha

## Leaves
- Shape: Ovate to broadly ovate
- Margin: Serrate to crenate
- Venation: Pinnate (prominent midrib with lateral veins)

## Bark
- Texture: Herbaceous (non-woody)
- Color: Green
- Changes with age: Stems remain soft; may become slightly fibrous before senescence

## Reproductive Structures
- Flowers / cones:
	- Monoecious
	- Inconspicuous green flowers in axillary spikes
	- male flowers distal ; female flowers proximal on the spike
- Fruit / seeds:
	- Small; 3-Lobed Capsules containing seeds

## Size & Form
- Typical height: 20-50 cm
- Crown shape: Low, spreading, bushy herb

## Habitat & Ecology
- Soil preference: Disturbed soils; loamy to sandy substrates
- Moisture: Moist to moderately dry
- Light: Full sun to partial shade

## Native Range
- Geographic distribution: East Asia (China, Korea, Japan)

## Similar Species
- [[Acalypha Indica]] 
- [[Acalypha Wilkesiana]]

## Notes
- Common Field Weed in cultivated areas and roadsides
- Frequent Host for [[Aecidium Acalyphae]]
- Lacks showy petals, identification relies on leaf shape and inflorescences
