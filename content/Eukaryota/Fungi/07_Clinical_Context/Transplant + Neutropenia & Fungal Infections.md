
## High-Risk Fungi
- [[Aspergillus Fumigatus]]
- [[Candida Albicans]]
- [[Mucorales]]

## Typical Presentations
- Fever unresponsive to antibiotics
- Pulmonary nodules
- Sinus invasion

## Key Imaging
- Halo sign (CT chest) → Aspergillus

## First-Line Treatments
- Aspergillosis → [[Azoles]] or echinocandins
- Mucormycosis → [[Polyenes]]

## Diagnostic Shortcut
> Neutropenic + lung nodules → **Aspergillus** until proven otherwise
