## Definition
Ability of a fungus to exist in two morphological forms depending on conditions.

## Common Trigger
- Temperature change

## Forms
- Mold form → environment
- Yeast form → host or nutrient-rich conditions

## Examples
- [[Histoplasma capsulatum]]
- [[Coccidioides Immitis]]

## Biological Advantage
Adaptation to different ecological niches.

## Note
Dimorphic switching is a biological survival mechanism, not primarily a pathogenic trait.
