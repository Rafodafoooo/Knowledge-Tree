# Rapid ID - Candida

## Given (lab clue)
- Morphology: Yeast
- Microscopy: Germ tube positive, pseudohyphae
- Culture: Creamy colonies on SDA
- Risk factors: Antibiotics, immunosuppression

## Most Likely Organism
- [[Candida Albicans]]

## Key Confirmatory Test
- Germ tube test

## First-Line Treatment
- [[Azoles]]

## One-Sentence Rule-Out
- Not Cryptococcus (no capsule, germ tube positive)
