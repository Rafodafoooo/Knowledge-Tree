## Core Components
- Chitin
- β-glucans
- Mannoproteins

## Chitin
- Polymer of N-acetylglucosamine
- Provides rigidity and structural strength
- Found in fungi and arthropods (not plants or mammals)

## β-glucans
- Structural polysaccharides
- Form a scaffold within the wall
- Important for wall integrity and shape

## Mannoproteins
- Outer layer proteins
- Involved in adhesion and immune interaction

## Key Biological Functions
- Maintains cell shape
- Protects against osmotic stress
- Anchors surface proteins

## Comparison
- Bacteria → peptidoglycan
- Plants → cellulose
- Fungi → chitin + glucans
