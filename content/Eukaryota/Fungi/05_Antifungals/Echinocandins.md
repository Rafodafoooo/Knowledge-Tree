# Echinocandins

## Mechanism of Action
Inhibit β-1,3-D-glucan synthesis → weakened fungal cell wall.

## Coverage (high yield)
- Effective against: Candida (including azole-resistant), Aspergillus
- Weak / no activity against: Cryptococcus, Mucorales

## Clinical Uses
- First-line for: invasive candidiasis

## Adverse Effects
- Generally well tolerated
- Mild infusion reactions
