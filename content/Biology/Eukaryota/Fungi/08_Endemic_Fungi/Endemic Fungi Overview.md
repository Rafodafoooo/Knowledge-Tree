## Core Rule
> Endemic fungi are **dimorphic** and acquired via **inhalation** from soil.

## Major Endemic Fungi
- [[Histoplasma capsulatum]] → Ohio & Mississippi River valleys
- [[Coccidioides Immitis]] → Southwestern US
- [[Blastomyces dermatitidis]] → Great Lakes & Midwest

## Exam Shortcut
> Geography + pneumonia symptoms → think endemic fungi
