# Azoles

## Mechanism of Action
Inhibit fungal cytochrome P450 → ↓ ergosterol synthesis → defective cell membrane.

## Coverage (high yield)
- Effective against: Candida spp., Cryptococcus, dimorphic fungi
- Weak / no activity against: Mucorales

## Clinical Uses
- First-line for: mucocutaneous candidiasis, many systemic mycoses

## Adverse Effects
- Hepatotoxicity
- QT prolongation
- Drug–drug interactions
