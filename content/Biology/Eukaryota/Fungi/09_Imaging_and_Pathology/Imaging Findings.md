## Lung Imaging

### Halo sign (CT chest)
- Ground-glass opacity surrounding a pulmonary nodule
- Suggests angioinvasion
- Think: [[Aspergillus Fumigatus]]

### Cavitary lung lesions
- Hemoptysis possible
- Think: [[Aspergillus Fumigatus]]

### Diffuse pulmonary infiltrates
- Especially in immunocompromised
- Think: [[Histoplasma capsulatum]]

---

## CNS Imaging

### Basilar meningitis
- Increased intracranial pressure
- Think: [[Cryptococcus Neoformans]]

---

## Sinus Invasion
- Black eschar, facial pain
- Think: [[Mucorales]]

## One-Look Rules
- Halo sign = Aspergillus
- Encapsulated yeast + CNS = Cryptococcus
- Black nasal eschar = Mucor
