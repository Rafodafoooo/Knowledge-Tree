## Core Concept
Fungi digest food externally and absorb the resulting small molecules.

## Process
1. Secrete extracellular enzymes
2. Break down complex polymers
3. Absorb simple nutrients across cell wall and membrane

## Contrast
- Animals → ingest food, digest internally
- Fungi → digest externally, absorb nutrients

## Biological Advantage
- Allows fungi to exploit solid substrates
- Enables growth within soil, wood, or organic debris
