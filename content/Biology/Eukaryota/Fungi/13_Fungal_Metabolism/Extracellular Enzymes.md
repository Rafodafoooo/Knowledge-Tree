## Purpose
Break down complex organic material outside the fungal cell.

## Common Enzymes
- Proteases → proteins
- Lipases → fats
- Amylases → starch
- Cellulases → plant cell walls

## Ecological Role
These enzymes allow fungi to act as:
- Decomposers
- Nutrient recyclers

## Structural Link
Enzyme secretion occurs primarily at:
- Hyphal tips
