# Mycelium

## Definition
A network of interconnected hyphae forming the body of a fungus.

## Functions
- Nutrient acquisition
- Structural support
- Environmental persistence

## Types
- Vegetative mycelium → nutrient absorption
- Aerial mycelium → spore production

## Notes
The mycelium is the primary biological form of molds.

## Metabolic Role
- Acts as a nutrient distribution network
- Supports sustained growth over large areas
