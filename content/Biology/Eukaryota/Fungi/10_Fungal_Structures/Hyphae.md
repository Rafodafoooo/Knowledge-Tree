# Hyphae

## Definition
Long, filamentous cells that make up the basic structural unit of molds.

## Types
- Septate hyphae → internal cross-walls
- Aseptate (coenocytic) hyphae → no septa

## Growth Pattern
- Apical growth (elongation at the tip)
- Allows rapid environmental invasion

## Examples
- Septate → [[Aspergillus Fumigatus]]
- Aseptate → [[Mucorales]]

## Why It Matters
Hyphal structure determines:
- Nutrient absorption
- Environmental spread
- Tissue invasion patterns

## Metabolic Significance
- Primary site of enzyme secretion
- Major interface for nutrient absorption
