
## High-Risk Fungi
- [[Candida Albicans]] → oral/esophageal candidiasis
- [[Cryptococcus Neoformans]] → meningitis
- [[Histoplasma capsulatum]] → disseminated disease

## Typical Presentations
- Oral thrush
- Dysphagia
- Headache + fever
- Subacute meningitis

## Key Lab Clues
- Encapsulated yeast (India ink) → Cryptococcus
- Antigen testing (serum/CSF)

## First-Line Treatments
- Cryptococcal meningitis → [[Polyenes]] + flucytosine
- Candidiasis → [[Azoles]]

## Diagnostic Shortcut
> HIV + CNS symptoms → think **Cryptococcus** until proven otherwise
