# Lab Identification Decision Tree

## Step 1: What do you see?
- Yeast → Step 2
- Mold → Step 3

## Step 2: Yeast
- Germ tube positive → [[Candida Albicans]]
- Capsule present → [[Cryptococcus Neoformans]]

## Step 3: Mold
- Septate, acute-angle branching → [[Aspergillus Fumigatus]]
- Broad, non-septate hyphae → [[Mucorales]]

## Step 4: Context clues
- Immunocompromised + bird/bat droppings → [[Histoplasma capsulatum]]
