## Yeast
- Budding yeast → [[Candida Albicans]]
- Encapsulated yeast (India ink) → [[Cryptococcus Neoformans]]

## Hyphae
- Septate hyphae with acute-angle branching → [[Aspergillus Fumigatus]]
- Broad, non-septate hyphae with right-angle branching → [[Mucorales]]

## Special Structures
- Germ tube positive → [[Candida Albicans]]
- Pseudohyphae → [[Candida Albicans]]
