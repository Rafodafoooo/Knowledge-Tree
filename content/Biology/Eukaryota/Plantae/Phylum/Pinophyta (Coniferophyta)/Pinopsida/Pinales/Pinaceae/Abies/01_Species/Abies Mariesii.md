# Abies Mariesii

## Quick ID (field-use)
- Leaf type: Evergreen Needle
- Leaf arrangement: Spirally Arranged on Shoots, Twisted at Base to Appear Two-Ranked
- One-liner ID clue: High-Elevation Japanese Fir with Bluish-Green, Flattened Needles and Erect Purple Cones

## Taxonomy
- Family: Pinaceae
- Genus: Abies

## Leaves
- Shape: Linear, Flattened Needles
- Margin: Entire (Smooth)
- Venation: Single Midvein (Not Visibly Reticulate)

## Bark
- Texture: Smooth when young; Fissured and scaly with age
- Color: Gray to Gray-Brown
- Changes with age: Develops vertical cracks and plates as tree matures

## Reproductive Structures
- Flowers / cones: 
	- Monoecious
	- Female Cones Erect, Cylindrical, Initially purple to dark violet
	- Male Cones Small, Yellowish to Reddish
- Fruit / seeds:
	- Winged Seeds released as cones disintegrate on the tree

## Size & Form
- Typical height: 15 - 30 m 
- Crown shape: Narrowly conical to spire shaped, dense branches

## Habitat & Ecology
- Soil preference: Well-Drained, Acidic Mountain Soil
- Moisture: Cool, Moist Environments, High Snowfall Regions
- Light: Shade-Tolerant when young, prefer full sun at maturity

## Native Range
- Geographic distribution:
	- Endemic to Japan
	- Primarily Northern and Central Honshu, Subalpine Zones

## Similar Species
- [[Abies Veitchii ]]
- [[Abies Sachalinensis]]

## Notes
- Dominant Species of Japanese Subapline Forests
- Adapted to Heavy Snow Load and Cold Climates
- Important Host for Several Rust Fungi, including [[Aecidium Abietis-Mariesii]]
